How to Read This
|β| > 1 — ELASTIC
1% price increase → more than 1% demand drop. Price-sensitive customers. Be careful raising prices.
|β| < 1 — INELASTIC
1% price increase → less than 1% demand drop. You have pricing power. Can raise prices with minimal volume loss.
R² — MODEL FIT
How well the log-log line fits your data. R² > 0.7 = reliable estimate. Lower = noisy data, interpret with caution.
OPTIMAL PRICE
The price point in your historical data that generated the highest total revenue. A starting point — not the final word.
